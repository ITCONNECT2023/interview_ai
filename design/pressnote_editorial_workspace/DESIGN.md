## Brand & Style
The design system is crafted for high-performing newsrooms, investigative desks, and journalistic teams who require seamless audio transcription, verified source auditing, and long-form article drafting. It embraces a refined **Modern Editorial Minimalist** aesthetic that merges the precision of a high-reliability SaaS workbench with the quiet focus of an editorial drafting room.

The visual tone conveys analytical rigor, transparency, and institutional authority. Unnecessary decorative pills, high-saturation fills, and noisy floating chrome are replaced by deliberate white space, quiet structural lines, and purposeful typographic cadence. The interface recedes into the background, prioritizing verification, transcript accuracy, and narrative composition.

## Colors
The color foundation is grounded in crisp, cool slate whites and architectural borders to provide maximum reading clarity during prolonged editorial sessions.

- **Primary (`#1e293b`)**: Deep journalistic slate utilized for primary controls, critical status headers, and authoritative framing.
- **Secondary (`#2563eb`)**: Precise cobalt blue designated for editorial interaction—active playback heads, text-to-audio scrubbing selections, interactive timecodes, and direct quote insertions.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Fact-checked verification green denoting confirmed attribution, verified quotes, and source corroboration.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`)**: Balanced slate gray for secondary metadata, audio track backgrounds, and descriptive captions.
- **Surface Architecture**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) is reserved for the composition canvas and transcript streams, layered over a crisp background canvas (`#f8fafc`) and framed by clean hairline rules (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Semantic Flags**: Amber (`#d97706`) signals AI transcription uncertainty or uncorroborated audio segments; Rose (`#e11d48`) isolates attribution contradictions and editorial blocks.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm is engineered to support deep reading and Korean bilingual fidelity without visual fatigue:

- **Noto Sans** drives all structural headings, body drafting, and transcript reading. It provides consistent stroke contrast and clear legibility across Korean syllables and Latin numerals.
- **JetBrains Mono** governs the temporal spine of the application: absolute timestamps, audio duration offsets, playback speeds, and speaker tags. Its fixed-width metric prevents alignment jitter during live playback.
- **Leading & Kerning**: Long-form draft composition uses a relaxed vertical stride (`32px` on `17px` font size) to give editorial eyes space for proofing and markup. Letter-spacing across Korean prose is set to `-0.01em` to mitigate wide glyph bounding boxes and maintain cohesive visual word units.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements an asymmetric 3-pane newsroom console engineered for continuous verification and composition:

- **Left Rail (Source & Audio Engine, 320px fixed)**: Contains the audio playback scrub bar, speaker roster, speech rate settings, and source file metadata.
- **Center Canvas (Synchronized Transcript, fluid flex-1)**: Hosts the chronological interview transcription with speaker segmentation, inline quote extraction anchors, and low-confidence flags.
- **Right Canvas (Composition Draft, max 736px / `draft-max-width`)**: A clean composition surface with generous padding (`2rem` to `2.5rem`) mimicking a publisher's manuscript desk.

### Adaptive Layout Behavior
- **Desktop Wide (≥1440px)**: Simultaneous three-pane arrangement where audio context, raw transcript, and draft sit side-by-side without overlapping drawers.
- **Standard Desktop & Laptop (1024px–1439px)**: The left rail collapses into an anchored persistent bottom audio dock (`audio-dock-height`), allowing the transcript feed and the article drafting canvas to split the remaining screen width equally.
- **Tablet / Compact (<1024px)**: Single-column view governed by a high-clarity segmented switcher between "Transcript Source" and "Article Draft", maintaining bottom dock access to audio playback.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the system is architectural and planar, relying on low-contrast structural boundaries and deliberate background tonal separation rather than heavy decorative shadows:

- **Level 0 (App Chassis)**: Background ground tone `#f8fafc`. Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Panels, Cards, and Canvases)**: `#ffffff` surfaces delineated by 1px `#e2e8f0` structural borders. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Hovered Paragraphs)**: Maintained on `#ffffff` with a crisp border accent `#cbd5e1` and a delicate micro-shadow (`0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Level 3 (Floating Editorial Toolbars & Selection Menus)**: `#ffffff` surface with a 1px `#cbd5e1` outline and a soft ambient shadow (`0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`), applied to quote-pull tooltips, playback speed menus, and citation popovers.
- **Level 4 (Audit Modals & Fact-Check Dialogs)**: `#ffffff` container surrounded by `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10)` with a subtle `#e2e8f0` border, anchored by an ambient backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)`).

## Shapes
The shape language is controlled and geometric, reflecting professional tool credibility:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Default for input fields, text buttons, toolbars, audio segment indicators, and table rows.
- **Container Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to self-contained cards, contextual panels, modal shells, and flyout menus.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Strictly restricted to real-time status badges (e.g., "Live Scrubbing", "Transcribing"), audio waveform scrubber thumbs, and speaker avatar tokens.

## Components

### Buttons & Transport Controls
- **Primary Actions (Publish, Export Article, Confirm Quote)**: Solid `#1e293b` fill, `#ffffff` text, 36px height, 14px horizontal padding, 4px corner radius. Hover shifts to `#0f172a`.
- **Secondary Actions (Save Draft, Align Audio)**: `#ffffff` fill, 1px `#e2e8f0` border, `#1e293b` text. On hover: `#f8fafc` background with `#cbd5e1` border.
- **Audio Transport Buttons**: 36px square controls with a 1px `#e2e8f0` border; playback needle toggle uses `#2563eb` fill when playing.
- **Text & Quote Anchor Actions**: Borderless `#2563eb` interactive icons that expose an instant "Pull into Draft" tooltip on hover.

### Transcript Paragraph Cards & Speaker Units
- **Speaker Block**: Header line featuring speaker label in `jetbrainsMono` bold uppercase (`#64748b`), followed by a clickable timestamp (`#2563eb`).
- **Interactive Transcript Line**: Clean padding (`12px 16px`) with a transparent 1px border. When audio reaches this sentence, background highlights to `#eff6ff` with a left border rule of 2px `#2563eb`.
- **Confidence Flagging**: Uncertain transcription spans feature a dashed 1px amber underline (`#d97706`). Clicking reveals an inline alternative suggestion box.

### Editorial Draft Editor
- **Canvas Styling**: Centered `draft-max-width` white card or fluid paper panel with `p-8` (32px) padding.
- **Blockquote Pull-ins**: 3px solid `#2563eb` left accent, indented text in `16px` with italic nuance, and an appended metadata chip linking back to the audio millisecond.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- **Verified Fact Chip**: `#ecfdf5` background, `#059669` text, 1px `#a7f3d0` border, `12px` font size, `2px 8px` padding.
- **Pending Review Chip**: `#fffbeb` background, `#d97706` text, 1px `#fde68a` border.
- **Contradiction Alert Chip**: `#fff1f2` background, `#e11d48` text, 1px `#fecdd3` border.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Transcript Search & Replace**: 36px height, crisp `#f8fafc` background that shifts to `#ffffff` on focus, bound by a 1px `#e2e8f0` border and a focused ring of 1px `#2563eb`. Placeholder text styled in `#94a3b8`.
